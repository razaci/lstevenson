ORIGIN STORY: THE EDITOR

EVERY GREAT CREATIVE WORLD NEEDS SOMEONE WHO SEES THE DETAILS.

Meet Lesley Stevenson — the editor helping bring Brian Chapman Sr.'s extraordinary range of creative projects into focus. Books, stories, games, programs, and digital experiences: her role is to help the work become clearer, stronger, and ready to share.

01!

THE STORY SHAPER

Big ideas deserve a strong presentation. Lesley helps turn sprawling creative material into something readers can follow, feel, and remember.

02!

THE DETAIL DETECTIVE

Names, flow, consistency, wording, pacing — the small choices that make a huge difference. An editor sees what the creator may miss.

03!

THE CREATIVE ALLY

Editing isn't about taking over someone's voice. It's about helping that voice come through more clearly — while keeping the creator's vision at the heart of the work.
